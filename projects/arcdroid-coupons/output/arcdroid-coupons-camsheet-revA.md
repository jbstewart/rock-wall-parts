| 2 | `HOLES_SMALL` | 1x 1/8" hole | **Left** | **In computer** | **in: 0.020 linear, perpendicular, radius 0** | **0.020 in (0.5 mm)** | none |
| 3 | `HOLES` | 4x 3/16, 1/4, 5/16, 3/8" | **Left** | **In computer** | stock | default 0.059 in (1.5 mm) | none |

Center comp on the squares is deliberate and is the whole point of op 1: the torch
runs ON the drawn line, so the slug comes out one kerf under and the hole one kerf
over, and `kerf = (H - S) / 2` cancels the drawn size. Do not "fix" it to Left.

0.020 pierce clearance on op 2 is the floor that lets a 1/8" hole generate with
lead-in on. Fusion silently DROPS any contour where lead-in plus clearance won't fit
inside the hole — a dropped contour is a hole that never gets cut, with no error.
After Generate, count the toolpaths against the contour counts in the table above.

## Measuring (runbook sections 5, 7, 8, 13)

Let it cool. Measure the **top face** — plasma kerf is wider at the top, and the top is
the face that shows. Keep away from the pierce and lead-out.

- Squares, each in X and Y: **S** across the slug (outside jaws), **H** across the hole
  left in the skeleton (inside jaws). `kerf = (H - S) / 2`, `path = (H + S) / 2`.
  Average kerf over all three; X and Y should agree within 0.005.
- Compare the 1" and 4" paths: same Y shortfall on both = a constant loss per contour.
  Four times the shortfall on the 4" = a scale error. Record both; do not fudge it in CAM.
- Holes: diameter in two directions, and look at the pierce. Round within 0.005 and on
  size within 0.010 passes. The floor is the smallest hole that passes.
- Flat bar: hole pitch 20 to 22 gives X error. Step in the line at the split
  gives Y error. Straightedge on the A half, gap at the far end of the B half, divide
  by 5, gives rotation.
