# ArcDroid cut-profile coupons — BRIEF

Current revision: **Rev A**

Two plasma test coupons that turn the ArcDroid from "it cuts" into a characterised machine. Neither is a part. Both exist to produce numbers that go into the record table in `areas/fabrication/ArcDroid-Cut-Profile-Runbook.md` (pp-operations), which is the spec for this job — sections 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13. That runbook says what the numbers are for; this job says what the steel looks like.

Everything is generated by `arcdroid_coupons.py`. Regenerate; don't hand-edit outputs.

## Why this job exists

Bonebrake was cut against a kerf nobody had measured and a machine nobody had characterised, and it showed: the 2" square coupon came out 2.000 in X and about 1.925 in Y, and a 39.625" hole span measured 3/32" short. Those are two different errors and the existing coupons can't tell them apart. These two can.

The rule this job is built on: **measure, don't compensate.** Nothing here is corrected in CAM. A machine error that gets fudged into the geometry is a machine error that ships with every future part.

## Coupon 1 — profile coupon

Material: **1/8" mild steel**, a drop that stays a drop. Blank **12.5 x 6**, X along the long side, origin at the lower-left corner. No outline is cut.

Three squares (1", 2", 4") on one centreline, 1" of web between them, and a column of five holes (1/8, 3/16, 1/4, 5/16, 3/8) on a 0.75" centre pitch to their right.

**Kerf, from the squares (runbook §7).** Measure each square twice in X and twice in Y, top face, away from the pierce: `S` across the slug with outside jaws, `H` across the hole left in the skeleton with inside jaws.

- `kerf = (H − S) / 2`
- `path = (H + S) / 2`

The torch centre runs one path and eats half a kerf either side, so the hole finishes one kerf over and the slug one kerf under. Drawn size and compensation both cancel — which is why three different square sizes can share one job, and why the squares op runs **Center** compensation on purpose.

**Constant vs scale error, from the 1" and 4" squares (runbook §5).** Compare the two paths:

- the **same** Y shortfall on both sizes is a constant loss per contour — backlash or arm deflection at reversal. Every part comes out short by that fixed amount whatever its size.
- **four times** the shortfall on the 4" square is a scale error. Parts and hole positions drift in proportion to size.

The distinction decides whether a hole pattern can be trusted, and it is an ArcDroid calibration item, not a Fusion one.

**Hole floor, from the column (runbook §8).** Measure each hole in two directions and look at the pierce. Round within 0.005 and on size within 0.010 passes; oval, teardropped at the pierce, or more than 0.010 under fails. The floor is the smallest hole that passes. Below the floor, holes get cut oversize and drilled, or center-marked and drilled — which is exactly the call Bonebrake had to make blind.

## Coupon 2 — flat-bar registration test

Material: **1/8" mild steel flat bar, 1.25 x 42**, X along the length, origin at the lower-left corner. No outline. Replaces the retired X2-Test.

Two 1/4" holes on the width centreline at X = 20 and X = 22, and one straight open line 0.25" from the lower long edge running X = 16 to 26 — five inches either side of the 21" split.

This one measures the X2 transition, where the ArcDroid slides and re-registers. Run it once per X2 setup, not once per profile, and after the skew correction (§13), because what it reads is what the skew correction left behind.

- **X pitch error** — the 20-to-22 hole pitch against a known 2.000.
- **Y step** — the offset in the line where it crosses the split.
- **Rotation** — straightedge on the A half of the line, measure the gap at the far end of the B half, divide by 5. Multiply by part height for the error at a far edge.

**The split is not in the geometry.** It is set in SimpleTrace at 533 mm. The line is drawn dead straight through that station deliberately: the only step in it will be the machine's.

## Blank size — why the profile coupon is not 6 x 10

The runbook asks for "about 6 by 10". It does not fit, and the arithmetic is not close:

| | |
|---|---|
| squares + webs | 1 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 4 = **9.000** |
| 1" margin each side | **11.000** of X before the hole column exists |
| 1" gap, then the 3/8" hole, then 1" margin | **12.375** minimum |
| rounded UP to the next 1/2" (house rule) | **12.500** |

So X grew from 10 to 12.5 and Y stayed at the spec'd 6.000, where the 4" square fills it exactly to its 1" margins. A drop that comes in a little under 6" tall just eats into that margin; it does not invalidate anything.

## The one relaxed rule

The 1" feature-to-feature clearance is **not** applied inside the hole column. The runbook fixes that column at a 0.75" pitch, so the two cannot both hold. The 1" rule is there to keep two large cutouts' heat fields and kerf error out of each other; inside the column the number that matters is the web between adjacent holes, and the generator checks that instead — minimum 0.406" (5/16 to 3/8), against a 3x-thickness floor of 0.375" and a two-kerf floor of 0.110". Squares, and square-to-hole, still hold the full 1".

## Layers

One sketch per layer on Insert DXF, so each op selects its own geometry and contours are never picked by hand.

| DXF | Layer | Contents | Op |
|---|---|---|---|
| profile coupon | `SQUARES` | 1, 2, 4" squares | Center comp, leads off |
| profile coupon | `HOLES_SMALL` | the 1/8" hole | Left, 0.020 pierce clearance |
| profile coupon | `HOLES` | 3/16, 1/4, 5/16, 3/8" | Left, default clearance |
| flat bar | `HOLES` | 2x 1/4" | Left, default clearance |
| flat bar | `LINE` | the open line | no comp, leads off |

Full settings are in the generated CAM sheet.

## Open items

- Both coupons are cut on the shop trip, after the SimpleTrace and firmware updates and the X2 skew correction — not before. An uncorrected skew makes the flat-bar numbers meaningless.
- Results go in three places the same day (runbook §11): the §12 record table, `docs/PROCESS.md`, and the SimpleTrace profile name.
- If the measured kerf moves off 1.4 mm, it goes in the Cutter tab of both tool copies and every ArcDroid op regenerates.
- Next profile is 3/16 steel: same profile coupon, tool swap, repost. The flat-bar test does not repeat.
