# Bonebrake artwork cut coupon — acceptance sheet (Rev D)

Generated by cutcoupon.py at Rev D — regenerate, don't hand-edit.
Program: `bonebrake-cutcoupon-revD.dxf`. Blank 8.500 x 12.000, 11ga mild steel, same lot as the panels.

## Why this exists

The generation gate says no run of too-thin material may exceed **0.957"** (8 x material thickness). That is reasoning about heat sinking, not a measurement. The artwork currently clears it 3.8x (longest steel run 0.252), but the limit itself has never been proven on this machine. Zone C locates it.

## Cut it the way the panel will be cut

- Same CAM setup as the panel programs (see the CAM sheet), same consumables
  at their current wear state — note the tip hours on this sheet.
- **Same cut ordering and the same back-to-back timing.** A coupon cut
  leisurely, with cooling between adjacent contours, tells you nothing about a
  panel cut continuously. This is the usual way coupons mislead.
- Same hold-down as the panel. Do not separate the zones.
- Orientation key: the clipped corner is TOP LEFT, art side up.

## Acceptance — measure, don't eyeball

1. **Ladder survival** per cell, table above.
2. **Flatness across the WHOLE coupon** — straightedge + feeler, record the
   worst gap and where. This is the one that scales to the panel: what kills
   the assembly is the face not seating on the frame, not an ugly cut.
3. **Local curl** at each surviving thin feature — a web can survive and still
   stand proud.
4. **Dross / tags** on the thin features, and whether they clean up without
   eating the feature.
5. **Detail loss** — compare zone A and B against the DXF. Tips will round;
   record how much, because that is what the customer sees.

## What this coupon cannot tell you

Cumulative distortion over 41.125". A 12.0" piece cannot reproduce the bow of a full panel. The coupon bounds the local question — do these features survive, and where is the limit. Whole-panel flatness is answered only by the panel, and the lever there is cut ordering in the dense band so adjacent contours are not run consecutively.
