# Truck cup holder — measurement worksheet

Replacement 3D-printed insert for the fold-down armrest dual cup holder. Fill in every blank; note "~" for anything estimated rather than measured. All dimensions in **inches** unless a field says otherwise (mm fine for the small clip/plate stuff — just write the unit).

Measure the OEM insert on a bench with calipers where possible; only the recess/plate items need to happen in the truck.

## 7. Decisions (circle/note)

- Removable insert (45° hook back-ramp, firm pull to remove) vs. semi-permanent (square hook)? → default: **removable**

- Bore depth target: as deep as the pocket allows, or shallower? → default: **max available minus 1/8" clearance**
